1. H1: isostatic gravity anomaly and slope: tilt-derivative zero-contour proximity gated by horizontal gradient; buried density-contrast boundaries may have no mapped surface rupture. Differs from existing U-Net/magnetic and ridge models by potential-field boundary location; low coding cost, uncertain gain. **Already implemented as transform, not real-data tested**.
2. H3: detrended elevation/slope and official 1 m DEM links: signed antisymmetric step response along scarps; low-amplitude scarps may have escaped regional map compilation. Differs from symmetric Sato ridge detection; medium cost, requires official tiles and coverage verification.
3. H2: geodetic dilatation/shear/second invariant: coherent strain corridors rather than mapped traces; buried active structures can deform without obvious outcrop. Differs from catalogue buffers; low cost, coarse resolution may erase signal.
4. H4: conductivity/depth-to-base: joint lateral-gradient alignment indicating fluid-altered fault zones; blind alteration may not appear in surface catalogue. Differs from magnetic-only edges; medium cost, ambiguous lithology confound.
5. H5: earthquake density and magnetic source depth: local density contrast near shallow basement edge; blind seismogenic structures can lack surface expression. Differs from seismicity alone; low cost, earthquake density may be spatially broad.

Ranking is a prioritization, not measured improvement. All require real training bands and labels for blocked evaluation.
