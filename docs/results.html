Degenerate baselines (what "doing nothing smart" scores)

Computed by src/gems/metric.py::degenerate_baselines against whatever truth is supplied. On the fixture grids: all-zero = 0.0000, catalogue-copy = 1.0000 (by construction). The informative numbers will be the real-band ones: all-zero (0.0), all-one-inside-footprint, and catalogue-copy — the last measures the "wrong target" trap (reproducing known faults scores 1.0 on the catalogue, ~0 on the hidden new faults).

Do not confuse sibling proxy numbers with ours. Figures quoted on sibling sites (blanket floor 0.0956, HGB proxy 0.1698, pindrop tables) are measurements on their checkouts against the public catalogue — a different population from the scored one. They are not reproduced here as facts; see flagged items.

Metric verification (self-tests)

  • Official worked example: TP=3.00, FP=1.89, FN=2.00 ⇒ DTI = 0.60 (scoring example) — asserted in tests/test_metric.py.
  • Hand-computed micro-grids: exact hit ⇒ DTI=1; 1-px offset ⇒ TP=2/3, FN=1/3, FP=1/3; 5-px offset ⇒ TP=0, FN=1, FP=1.
  • Known-fault masking: prediction on a masked pixel contributes 0 to FP (both readings measured).
  • Validator self-test: GOOD file 13/13 PASS; NaN-inside ⇒ gate 12 FAIL; value>1 ⇒ gate 9 FAIL; finite-outside ⇒ gate 13 FAIL.
